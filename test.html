CAPTAIN AMERICA

Captain America, whose real name is Steve Rogers, stands as one of the most enduring and iconic figures in comic book history. Created by Joe Simon and Jack Kirby, he first burst onto the cultural landscape in Captain America Comics #1 in March 1941—several months before the United States officially entered World War II. Draped in the stars and stripes, the character was explicitly designed as a patriotic sentinel against the rising tide of fascism and the Axis powers, famously punching Adolf Hitler on the cover of his very first issue. At his core, Steve Rogers was not born a hero with extraordinary powers. Originally a frail, sickly artist from Brooklyn, New York, he faced repeated rejections when trying to enlist in the U.S. Army due to his numerous health issues and physical frailty. However, his unyielding moral compass, fierce determination, and refusal to back down from bullies caught the attention of Dr. Abraham Erskine. Erskine selected Rogers for the top-secret Operation Rebirth. Subjected to a cocktail of experimental chemicals and "Vita-Rays," Rogers was transformed into a human specimen of peak physical perfection—gaining towering height, incredible strength, agility, and reflexes.
