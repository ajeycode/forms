ABOUT- BATMAN THE BEGINING

Gotham City is a dark, crowded place where crime and corruption threaten everyday life. Its tall buildings, busy streets, and shadowy alleys create an atmosphere of mystery. Watching over the city is Batman, a hero who uses intelligence, courage, and advanced technology to fight criminals. Unlike many superheroes, Batman has no superpowers. He relies on careful planning, detective skills, gadgets, and determination. With help from trusted allies, he works to protect Gotham’s people and bring hope to the city. Batman’s story shows that even in difficult circumstances, one person can choose to stand up for what is right.
